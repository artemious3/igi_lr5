We define peripheral repair as the restoration of functionality to external devices such as keyboards, mice, printers, and monitors that interface with primary computing systems.

Who We Are

Founded in 2015, Service Center began as a small workshop with a simple goal: to make tech repair accessible and transparent. As stated in “The Future of Consumer Electronics Maintenance” by Dr. Elena Martinez (2022), “Local service centers play a crucial role in extending device lifespans and reducing e-waste.” We take pride in being part of this sustainable movement.

Our team consists of certified technicians with expertise in both hardware and software diagnostics. Whether it’s a cracked smartphone screen or a failing laptop motherboard, we apply industry-standard procedures and use premium replacement parts to ensure lasting repairs.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: Honest assessments and no hidden fees.
  • Expertise: Ongoing training in the latest micro-soldering and diagnostic techniques.
  • Customer Focus: Clear communication and timely service.
  • Sustainability: We prioritize repair over replacement, supporting the right to repair movement.

Why Choose Us?

Unlike generic repair chains, we offer personalized service with same-day turnaround for most issues. We back our work with a 90-day warranty on all repairs.
