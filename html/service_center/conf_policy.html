Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

We may collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and details about your device and repair request. This information is necessary to provide our services and communicate with you.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Improve our services and customer support.

3. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We use secure servers and encryption to keep your information safe.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (e.g., payment processors) to assist with our operations. These third parties have their own privacy policies and may have access to your information only to the extent necessary to perform their functions.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. If you have any questions about our privacy practices, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
